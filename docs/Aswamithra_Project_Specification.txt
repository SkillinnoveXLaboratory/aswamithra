1. Project Overview
Aswamithra is a location-based online marketplace — similar in speed and convenience to Zepto — that directly connects farmers with nearby customers and bulk (B2B) buyers. Customers open the app or website, set their location and a search radius, and instantly see all verified farmers and their products (rice, vegetables, grains, etc.) available around them. Farmers list their own products, set their own prices and units, and receive payments directly to their bank accounts. The platform earns through a small, admin-controlled commission on every transaction.
1.1 Goals
	•	Remove middlemen so farmers earn more and customers pay less.
	•	Give every farmer a simple digital shop with full control over prices, stock, and units (kg, grams, units, tons, quintal).
	•	Show customers exactly how much money they saved versus market price, and show farmers exactly how much extra they earned by selling through Aswamithra.
	•	Give the Admin team full control: commission rates, shop creation by location, farmer verification, billing, banners, and analytics across India.
	•	Support three separate login types — Customer, Farmer, B2B — plus a secure Admin panel.
1.2 Key Differentiators
	•	Radius-based discovery: buy from farmers within 5 / 10 / 25 km of your location (powered by Mapbox + PostGIS).
	•	Dynamic commission engine: e.g., 4.5% on transactions up to Rs. 10,000 and 4% above — fully editable by Admin without code changes.
	•	Transparency dashboards: "You saved Rs. X" for customers, "You earned Rs. X via Aswamithra" for farmers.
	•	Admin-created shops location-wise across India, with a proper billing and invoicing system.

3.1 Why PostgreSQL and not Firebase as the main database
	•	Your core feature is a geographic radius search. PostGIS answers "all farmers within 10 km, sorted by distance" in one fast query; Firestore needs geohash workarounds.
	•	PostgreSQL is 100% free on your VPS. Firestore charges per read/write and marketplace browsing generates huge read volumes.
	•	Future plan stays intact: the same PostgreSQL later moves to Google Cloud SQL, and Firebase is used only for FCM push, Crashlytics, and Analytics (its genuinely best parts).

4. Registration & Onboarding (Step by Step)
4.1 Common First Steps (all roles)
	•	Visitor opens the website/app and sees the welcome screen with hovering banner images and a "Join as: Customer / Farmer / B2B" choice.
	•	User enters their mobile number. Backend sends a 6-digit OTP (via MSG91 through Supabase Auth). OTP expires in 5 minutes; maximum 3 resends; rate-limited by Redis to stop abuse.
	•	User enters OTP. On success, a verified account shell is created with the chosen role.
	•	Optionally the user can also link Gmail (Google Sign-In) for one-tap future logins. Email is verified via Google, so no separate email OTP is needed.
	•	User sets a 4-digit app PIN or password as a second login method.

4.2 Customer Registration Details
	•	Full name, mobile (verified), email (optional Gmail link).
	•	Delivery address: house/street, landmark, pincode, city, state.
	•	Location pin on Mapbox (auto-detect GPS with manual adjust) — stored as latitude/longitude for radius search.
	•	Preferred language (Telugu / Hindi / English).
Customer account becomes active immediately and lands on the home screen.
4.3 Farmer Registration Details
	•	Full name, mobile (verified), email (optional), profile photo.
	•	Farm location pin on Mapbox + village, mandal/taluk, district, state, pincode.
	•	KYC: Aadhaar number + Aadhaar photo upload; optional PAN.
	•	Bank details for payouts: account holder name, account number, IFSC (verified later with a Rs. 1 penny-drop via Razorpay).
	•	Crops/products grown, approximate land size, farm photos (builds customer trust).
Status = Pending. Admin reviews KYC in the Admin panel and Approves or Rejects (with reason). Farmer gets SMS + WhatsApp on approval and can then list products.
4.4 B2B Registration Details
	•	Business name, owner name, mobile (verified), business email.
	•	GSTIN (validated format), business type (retailer / hotel / mill / wholesaler).
	•	Business address + location pin, trade license or shop registration upload.
	•	Optional: expected monthly purchase volume (helps Admin offer credit terms).
Status = Pending until Admin verifies GSTIN and documents. After approval, B2B catalog with bulk pricing unlocks.
4.5 Login Methods (all roles)
	•	Mobile + OTP (primary).
	•	Gmail one-tap (if linked).
	•	Mobile + PIN/password (fallback).
	•	Session tokens (JWT) with refresh; auto-logout of Admin sessions after 30 minutes of inactivity.

5. Customer Panel — Complete Flow
5.1 Home Screen
	•	Personalised welcome note ("Good morning, Ravi!") and hovering/carousel banner images managed by Admin.
	•	Location bar showing current delivery location with a radius selector: 5 km / 10 km / 25 km / custom.
	•	Category tiles: Rice & Grains, Vegetables, Fruits, Pulses, Dairy, Others.
	•	Footer on every page: all official social media links (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, WhatsApp channel) and an India map/list of active locations — both managed from the Admin panel.
5.2 Discovery (the core feature)
	•	Customer sets/confirms location (GPS or manual pin on Mapbox).
	•	Backend runs a PostGIS query: find all approved farmers whose farm point lies within the chosen radius, with in-stock products, sorted by distance.
	•	Results screen shows farmer cards: photo, name, village, distance (e.g., "3.2 km away"), rating, and their product list.
	•	Customer can also search by product ("rice bag") — results show every farmer in radius selling rice, with price per unit for comparison.

5.3 Product Page
	•	Product photos, name, description, price, and unit exactly as the farmer set them (Rs./kg, Rs./quintal, Rs./unit, etc.).
	•	Market reference price (maintained by Admin per product-category-region) shown with a strike-through, so the saving is visible before buying.
	•	Farmer mini-profile: photo, farm photos, rating, total orders served, distance.
	•	Quantity selector respecting the farmer’s minimum order quantity and live stock.
5.4 Cart & Checkout
	•	Add to cart (multiple farmers allowed; cart groups items per farmer since each farmer fulfils separately).
	•	Checkout: confirm address, choose delivery slot, apply coupon (if Admin has published any).
	•	Payment via Razorpay: UPI / card / netbanking / wallets, or Cash on Delivery if Admin has enabled COD for that region.
	•	Order confirmation screen + SMS/WhatsApp/push with order ID.

5.5 Order Tracking & After-Sales
	•	Live status: Placed → Farmer Accepted → Packed → Out for Delivery → Delivered.
	•	After delivery: rate the farmer (1–5 stars + comment) and report issues (opens a dispute the Admin team handles).
	•	Reorder in one tap from order history.
5.6 Customer Savings Dashboard
For every delivered order the system stores (market reference price − price paid) × quantity into a savings ledger. The dashboard shows:
	•	"You saved Rs. 1,240 this month by buying directly from farmers."
	•	Lifetime savings, monthly graph, and savings per order in the invoice.

6. Farmer Panel — Complete Flow
6.1 Dashboard
	•	Welcome note with today’s summary: new orders, pending deliveries, today’s earnings.
	•	Headline card: "You earned Rs. X extra by selling through Aswamithra" — calculated as (Aswamithra sale price − local mandi reference price) × quantity, accumulated in an earnings ledger.
	•	Low-stock alerts and rating summary.
6.2 Product Management
	•	Add Product: name, category, photos, description.
	•	Set price and choose unit from: Kg, Grams, Units (pieces), Tons, Quintal. The farmer fully controls the price.
	•	Set available stock, minimum order quantity, and optional bulk price tiers for B2B (e.g., cheaper per quintal).
	•	Publish — the product instantly appears to all customers whose radius covers this farm.
	•	Edit price/stock anytime; pause a product with one tap (e.g., sold out at the farm).

6.3 Order Handling
	•	New order alert arrives by push notification + WhatsApp + SMS.
	•	Farmer opens the order: items, quantity, customer area, payout amount after commission clearly shown.
	•	Accept or Reject within a time window (e.g., 30 minutes; auto-cancel with refund if no response).
	•	Mark as Packed → handover to delivery (own delivery / platform partner / pickup point, per the region’s delivery model).
	•	On Delivered: payment auto-settles.

6.4 Payments & Payouts
	•	Online orders: Razorpay Route automatically splits every payment — farmer’s share goes to the farmer’s linked bank account, commission goes to the platform account. No manual settlement.
	•	COD orders: delivery partner collects; platform settles the farmer’s share in the next payout cycle (T+2).
	•	Payout statement screen: every order, gross amount, commission %, commission amount, net credited, UTR number, date.
6.5 Earnings Dashboard
	•	"Earned via Aswamithra: Rs. X" (extra earnings vs. mandi reference price).
	•	Total sales, orders completed, best-selling products, monthly trend graph.
	•	Downloadable monthly statement (PDF) for the farmer’s records.

7. B2B Panel — Complete Flow
7.1 Catalog & Ordering
	•	Bulk catalog with quintal/ton pricing tiers set by farmers (or negotiated via quotes).
	•	Two paths: (a) Direct bulk order at listed tier price, or (b) Request For Quote — B2B sends required quantity, nearby farmers (or Admin on their behalf) respond with an offer, B2B accepts.
	•	Minimum order values and delivery scheduling (choose a delivery date; bulk needs planning).
7.2 Payments, Invoicing & Credit
	•	Payment options: full advance, partial advance + balance on delivery, or credit terms (only if Admin has approved a credit limit for that B2B account).
	•	Automatic GST invoice generated for every order (GSTIN of both parties, HSN codes, tax breakup) — legally required for B2B trade.
	•	Credit ledger: outstanding amount, due dates, payment history, auto-reminders on WhatsApp/email.
7.3 B2B Dashboard
	•	Purchase history, GST reports (monthly, downloadable for filing), savings vs. wholesale market rate, repeat-order shortcuts.

8. Admin Panel — Complete Flow
8.1 Access & Roles
	•	Separate secure URL with mandatory 2-factor login (password + OTP).
	•	Sub-roles: Super Admin (everything), Operations (KYC, shops, orders), Finance (commissions, payouts, refunds), Support (disputes, customers).
	•	Every admin action is written to an audit log (who, what, when).
8.2 Verification (KYC) Queue
	•	Open pending Farmer/B2B applications; view uploaded Aadhaar/GSTIN/licenses side by side with entered details.
	•	Approve, Reject (with reason sent by SMS/WhatsApp), or Request re-upload.
	•	Bank account check: trigger Rs. 1 penny-drop verification through Razorpay before enabling payouts.

8.6 Content & Marketing Controls
	•	Banner manager: upload hovering/carousel images, welcome notes, set start/end dates, target audience (customers or farmers).
	•	Social media link manager: add/edit Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, WhatsApp links shown in the footer of the website and app.
	•	Locations page manager: mark active districts/cities across India.
	•	Coupon engine: create discount codes with limits.
8.7 Disputes & Refunds
	•	Dispute queue from customer complaints; Admin can issue full/partial refund via Razorpay, penalise repeat-offending sellers, or reject with explanation.

10.1 The Radius Query (heart of the app)
SELECT f.*, ST_Distance(f.location, :customer_point) AS dist
FROM farmer_profiles f
WHERE f.kyc_status = 'approved'
  AND ST_DWithin(f.location, :customer_point, :radius_meters)
ORDER BY dist;
With a GIST index on location, this answers in milliseconds even with lakhs of farmers.

11. Payment & Commission Flow (End to End)
	•	Customer pays Rs. 12,000 for a rice order via Razorpay.
	•	Backend looks up commission_slabs: amount is above Rs. 10,000 → rate = 4.0% → commission = Rs. 480.
	•	Razorpay Route splits instantly: Rs. 11,520 routed to the farmer’s linked account, Rs. 480 to the platform account.
	•	payments table stores both splits; farmer’s payout statement and Admin’s finance report update automatically.
	•	savings_ledger and earnings_ledger entries are created from the market reference price, updating both dashboards.

	•	Refunds reverse both splits proportionally through Razorpay.
	•	COD: commission is deducted from the next payout cycle instead.
	•	All rates, slabs, and even per-region overrides are Admin-editable at any time; historical orders keep their original rate.

12. Security & Data Protection
	•	OTP: 5-minute expiry, max 3 attempts, Redis rate-limiting per number and per IP.
	•	Passwords/PINs hashed with bcrypt; JWT access tokens (15 min) + refresh tokens; Admin sessions expire after 30 minutes idle.
	•	HTTPS everywhere (Let’s Encrypt); Aadhaar/bank documents stored encrypted in Supabase storage with access only via signed URLs.
	•	Role-based API guards: a farmer token can never call customer or admin endpoints.
	•	Daily automatic PostgreSQL backups to off-server storage; 30-day retention.
	•	Audit log of every admin action; payment webhooks verified with Razorpay signatures.
	•	Compliance note: store only masked Aadhaar (last 4 digits) in the database; full document only as an encrypted file, per Indian data-protection norms.

13. Deployment on Your VPS
Everything runs as Docker containers behind Nginx on the VPS you already purchased:
nginx (SSL, Let’s Encrypt)
  ├─ nextjs-web        (customer/farmer/b2b website)
  ├─ nestjs-api        (all backend APIs)
  ├─ admin-panel       (Refine/React-Admin build)
  ├─ supabase          (auth, OTP, file storage — self-hosted)
  ├─ postgresql+postgis (main database)
  └─ redis             (cache, rate limits, queues)

	•	One docker-compose file starts the whole platform; updates deploy with zero downtime.
	•	Daily cron: database backup + upload to external storage.
	•	Monitoring: Uptime Kuma (open source) for alerts if anything goes down.
13.1 Future Move to Firebase/Google Cloud (Your Plan)
	•	Phase now: everything on VPS as above. Add Firebase FCM immediately (free) for push notifications.
	•	Phase scale: lift the same PostgreSQL to Google Cloud SQL, containers to Cloud Run; keep Firebase for FCM, Crashlytics, Analytics. No rewrite — because all database access sits behind a repository layer in the code (instruct your developer to enforce this from day one).

15. Important Items You Should Not Miss
	•	Delivery model decision (most critical): farmer self-delivery, hired delivery partners per district, or customer pickup points. Zepto-style speed needs local delivery capacity — plan this before launch.
	•	Multi-language UI (Telugu / Hindi / English) — essential for farmer adoption.
	•	Farmer training: a simple WhatsApp video guide on adding products and accepting orders.
	•	Weight/quality verification step for B2B bulk orders (photo/weighbridge slip upload at dispatch).
	•	Legal: platform Terms & Conditions, privacy policy, refund policy, GST registration for the platform itself, and FSSAI if you handle food storage in your own shops.
	•	Customer support channel: WhatsApp support number + in-app help, managed from the Admin Support role.
	•	Seasonal price volatility: allow Admin to update market reference prices weekly so savings/earnings numbers stay honest.
