Cooking Instruction

01 - Blend the Batter
Add bananas, oats, egg, milk, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt to a blender. Blend until smooth and thick.
02 - Heat the Pan
Warm a nonstick skillet over medium heat for 1–2 minutes. Lightly coat it with a small amount of butter or oil to prevent sticking.
03 - Cook the Pancakes
Spoon batter into the pan in rounds. Cook until bubbles form on top (about 2–3 mins), then flip and cook for another 1–2 mins until golden.
